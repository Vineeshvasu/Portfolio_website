Overview

The Challenge

In a hub-and-spoke hospital network, critical cardiac cases from smaller spoke hospitals needed fast, coordinated handoff to the central hub. But fragmented tools — phone calls, WhatsApp, standalone ECG apps — created dangerous gaps in information.

Doctors at spoke hospitals had no way to share ECGs, case notes, and patient context to the hub in a structured, trackable way. One Mobile needed to unify ECG acquisition, criticality tagging, and real-time collaboration across all 8 hospitals in the network.

Design Process

Designing Across Roles

01 — Field Research

Visited 4 spoke hospitals and the hub. Observed night-shift handover routines and mapped the full case journey from ECG capture to transfer decision.

Uncovered 6 critical handoff failure points — all stemming from fragmented communication tools, not clinical knowledge gaps.

02 — Role Mapping

Designed for 3 distinct roles: Spoke Doctor (refer), Hub Cardiologist (review + approve), Admin (monitor). Each role had a tailored view and action set within the same app.

3-role architecture validated with 9 doctors and 2 hospital admins across hub and spoke sites.

03 — Design

Built 60+ screens across role-specific flows. Designed a criticality tagging system with 4 levels based on clinical guidelines validated by cardiologists.

Criticality system approved by senior cardiologist panel — aligned with national STEMI care protocols.

04 — Pilot & Iterate

Piloted with 2 hospitals, collected in-app feedback, and ran fortnightly iteration cycles for 8 weeks before full 8-hospital rollout.

14 design iterations over 8 weeks. 3 major flow changes based on real doctor behaviour during the pilot.
