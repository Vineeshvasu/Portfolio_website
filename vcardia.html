Overview

The Challenge

Portable 12-lead ECG devices existed, but pairing them with a seamless digital experience was an unmet need. Clinicians struggled with complex setup flows, lost recordings, and no way to sync data to the cloud reliably.

VCardia needed to feel as simple as a consumer app while meeting the reliability demands of clinical hardware. The SDK additionally had to expose a clean developer interface for third-party integrations.

My Approach

How I Approached It

I designed for failure first. Before the happy path, I mapped what happens when the battery is low, the signal is noisy or the network drops, so the app stays calm and helpful when hardware misbehaves.

VCardia also had two audiences: clinicians using the app and developers integrating the SDK. I designed both experiences side by side so they shared the same mental model.

Vision & Innovation

The Vision

Make recording a clinical-grade 12-lead ECG as simple as pairing a pair of earbuds. Recordings sync to the cloud reliably and reports come back instantly, while the SDK lets partners build the same experience into their own products.

Identifying Unique Challenges

What Made It Hard

  • Bluetooth pairing: Pairing was the most common failure point. Users had no idea what state the device was in or what to do next.
  • Signal quality feedback: Technicians couldn't tell whether a recording was good until it was too late to retake it.
  • Upload confidence: Without clear confirmation, users didn't know if a recording had reached the cloud, which risked lost data.
  • Two audiences: The consumer UI had to feel effortless while the SDK exposed a clean, predictable interface for third parties.
Resolving Complex Problems

Design Solutions

Guided pairing with live states

Step-by-step pairing with animated status, prototyped in Principle, shows exactly what the device is doing and what to do next.

Zero re-pairing required between sessions after the redesign.

Real-time signal feedback

Live signal-quality cues during acquisition let technicians fix electrode issues before the recording finishes.

Designed from 12 mapped failure scenarios across device states.

Clear sync and upload status

Every recording shows an explicit status, from captured to uploading to synced to report ready, so nothing gets lost silently.

Zero data-loss incidents after launch.

Developer-friendly SDK

SDK documentation and flows were designed alongside the app, giving partners a consistent, well-documented integration path.

3 SDK partners integrated in the first year.

Design Process

From Device to Cloud

01 — Discovery

Observed technicians using competitor devices in field settings. Identified 3 critical failure points: Bluetooth pairing, signal quality feedback, and upload confirmation.

Mapped 12 distinct failure scenarios across device states — low battery, poor signal, and network loss.

02 — Map

Mapped the full device-to-report journey across hardware states. Designed for error conditions first, then the happy path — ensuring robustness was baked into every interaction.

End-to-end flow documented across 4 hardware states and 3 network conditions.

03 — Design

Designed both the consumer mobile UI and the SDK documentation. Used Principle to prototype Bluetooth pairing micro-animations for testing.

50+ screens covering the full acquisition, sync, and report flows across iOS and Android.

04 — Validate

Ran 3 rounds of device-in-hand usability testing with nurses and technicians. Measured setup time from unbox to first successful recording.

Setup time reduced from 40 minutes to under 10. Zero re-pairing required between sessions post-redesign.
Accessibility & Optimisation

Built for Everyone

  • Status in words: device, signal and upload states always pair colour with text and icons.
  • Error-state design: every failure scenario has a clear recovery message instead of a dead end.
  • Cross-platform consistency: matching patterns on iOS and Android reduce retraining across teams.
  • Optimised setup: setup time dropped from about 40 minutes to under 10.
