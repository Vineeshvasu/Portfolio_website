Overview

The Challenge

Portable 12-lead ECG devices existed, but pairing them with a seamless digital experience was an unmet need. Clinicians struggled with complex setup flows, lost recordings, and no way to sync data to the cloud reliably.

VCardia needed to feel as simple as a consumer app while meeting the reliability demands of clinical hardware. The SDK additionally had to expose a clean developer interface for third-party integrations.

Design Process

From Device to Cloud

01 — Discovery

Observed technicians using competitor devices in field settings. Identified 3 critical failure points: Bluetooth pairing, signal quality feedback, and upload confirmation.

Mapped 12 distinct failure scenarios across device states — low battery, poor signal, and network loss.

02 — Map

Mapped the full device-to-report journey across hardware states. Designed for error conditions first, then the happy path — ensuring robustness was baked into every interaction.

End-to-end flow documented across 4 hardware states and 3 network conditions.

03 — Design

Designed both the consumer mobile UI and the SDK documentation. Used Principle to prototype Bluetooth pairing micro-animations for testing.

50+ screens covering the full acquisition, sync, and report flows across iOS and Android.

04 — Validate

Ran 3 rounds of device-in-hand usability testing with nurses and technicians. Measured setup time from unbox to first successful recording.

Setup time reduced from 40 minutes to under 10. Zero re-pairing required between sessions post-redesign.
